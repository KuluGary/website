Who is the webmaster?

Work

My primary experience is build web applications, both client-facing and for internal management. I thrive with back-of-the-front-end development, specializing in business logic, integrating with internal and third-party APIs, and working with other IT profiles to ensure information safety and solid data contracts.

Of the projects I've worked on, a few are:

  • A platform to help retail stores to manage their digital content in each point-of-sale. The core system includes a dashboard to upload, manage and send the content to each display application.
  • An application to be displayed in hotel lobbies to manage check-ins and check-outs. Displayed in kiosk mode in custom hardware.
  • A platform to create social media content. It connects freelance editors and filmers with clients, manages uploading and downloading content and provides a secure payment system.

Life on the Internet

I've been on the Internet one way or another since the mid-2000s. Before social media was a centralized mess, I was lurking in some obscure gaming forums, where I first learned about game development.

I started overscoped games in RPG Maker when I was a teenager, from fangames to full-on fantasy epics that did not get more than a couple demos before being abandoned for another idea.

At some point I co-created an RPG Maker forum with some of my friends, which I moderated until the mid 2010s.

After that, I was invited to a RP forum by a friend. In there I first learned about TTRPGs.
