Hi, I'm Gary and I'm the curator, author and webmaster of {{ metadata.title }}.

Work

I work as a full-time web developer in both client-facing and management applications. I specialize in the back-of-the-front-end, integrating APIs, managing business logic and working with both designers and backend developers to ensure product quality.

Some of the projects I've worked on are:

  • A centralized platform that enables retailers to manage and automate multimedia content across physical locations. It supported synchronized playback of audio, video, and scent diffusion, allowing consistent and programmable in-store experiences across multiple points of sale.
  • A platform that connects brands with freelance videographers and editors to produce high-quality video content on demand. It manages the full production workflow, from briefing and professional matching to recording, editing, revisions, and delivery, all within a centralized interface.

Life on the Internet

I've been on the Internet one way or another since the mid-2000s. Before people congregated into centralized social medias, I was part of some obscure game-developer forums where I first started sharing my games.

Around 2011 I first discover RP forums. There I started to create my first fiction stories with my partner, some of them which we're still working on to this day.

Hobbies

I'm an eclectic person with a lot of interests, and I've shared quite a few of them with the Internet.

I've shared my art, my games, some videos, some code and lots of stuff I've yet to post. I have an archive of old stuff I've shared over the years, if you want to check it out.
