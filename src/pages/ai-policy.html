AI Policy

This policy refers to the use of “Artificial Intelligence” (aka “Generative AI”, “LLMs”) in the making of this website. I use the acronym “AI” to refer to any and all of these technologies.

I do not use generative AI to write or edit the articles published on this website. I also do not use generative AI to program or maintain the website.

The topics covered by this website are researched and written without using generative AI as a source or author.

Some articles may link to or embed content from other websites. I do not control how those websites create their content, and I cannot guarantee that material published by third parties is free from AI-generated or AI-assisted content.

Any views, research, writing, and conclusions presented in my articles are my own, unless otherwise stated.
