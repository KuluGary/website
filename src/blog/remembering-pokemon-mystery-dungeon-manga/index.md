When I was a kid, I was really into videogame and cartoon magazines. I was lucky and my neighborhood was full of kiosks and they always had lots of them on sale. I ended up collecting quite a lot, both player guides and gaming news.

One collectibe I still own to this day and I remember fondly is a small supplement that came with the Jetix Magazine. It was Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Ginji's Rescue Team –or ポケモン不思議のダンジョンギンジの救助隊 in the original japanese. Unlike the manga I was used to reading which came in tankōbon, these were made into little A6 booklets with a single chapter on each.

At that point in time, I had played other Pokémon games but not Mystery Dungeon, so it was a big shock that the protagonist and friends were Pokémon! Unlike most Pokémon games where the story is fairly straightforward, this manga depicted personal stakes and a big mystery. How can a human be transformed into a Pokémon and transported into their world!?

The first chapter of the manga, *Pokémon to the Rescue!* (<span>ポケモンになった<ruby>少年<rp>(</rp><rt>しょうねん</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></span>), introduces the reader to Ginji, a [hasty](https://mysterydungeonwiki.com/wiki/Rescue_Team:Nature#Hasty) kid excited to get home after class to try and find his birthday present, hidden by his mom at home. He eventually passes out from exhaustion, muttering “I won’t give up… Until I find… My p-present…”. When he wakes up though, he finds out he has been transformed into a Torchic!

The art on this manga is very cool. Unlike some other versions of these characters, Mr. Makoto Mizobuchi really accomplishes giving a threatening and intense look into the Pokémon. He is the mangaka behind the Pokémon Adventures manga too, and his experience shows in the quality of the artwork.

{% image category, postSlug %}
zapdos.png | Zapdos is really scary in this manga.
{% endimage %}

Even before playing the games, this manga left a big impression on me. I remember making a fan-story with my own Pokémon characters and gifting hand-made booklets to my friends in the schoolyard. My mom must have spent a small fortune in photocopies at the print shop…

After that I got to play Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team on my Game Boy Advance. I loved the game, but I must have restarted the personality quiz at least half a dozen times so I could get to play as Torchic.

Is this manga why my favorite Pokémon of all time is this small fire chicken!?
