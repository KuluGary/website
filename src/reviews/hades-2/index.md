After spending 100+ hours playing the first Hades, I bought Hades II when it was still in early access and I don't regret it. Everything that made the first game amazing comes back with a force.

Even though I have more than double the hours in Hades II than its predecessor, I think I consider the first one its superior. Its more fast-paced gameplay, more balanced difficulty, simpler secondary systems and tighter narrative storyline still puts Hades I above the sequel in my opinion.

Nevertheless, it's still an amazing game I can't recommend enough to both fans of action games, great stories and roguelites.

{# Even though it's a great game, I have a couple main complaints that in my opinion puts this game a bit behind its predecessor.

First, the progression in complexity from the first game to this one makes it so that there are more systems in place. Not only there are multiple routes to progress the story, the amount of secondary currency, progression mechanics and the depth of the Arcana system versus the first one's Mirror of Night makes it so that it is a complicated game.

Then, Melinöe doesn't play like Zagreus. This may be obvious, but Melinöe controls very similarly to Zag, but when you have played a while you start noticing the subtle but very core differences. Hades I promotes aggressive play, with a lot of the weapons having a fast playstyle where you constantly dash in and out while spamming attacks. Melinöe on the other hand is much slower, both in how her Dash works and the attack speed of most of her weapons, which promotes a much more careful and measured playstyle.

Lastyl I feel like the story evolver to be a bit flatter than in Hades I. The core conflict of the previous story worked perfectly with the gameplay premise, but in Hades II the story feels much more forced to adapt to the roguelite premise. #}
