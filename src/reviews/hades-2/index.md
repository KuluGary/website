After spending 100+ hours playing the first Hades, I bought Hades II when it was still in early access and I don't regret it. Everything that made the first game amazing comes back with a force.

Even though I have more than double the hours in Hades II than its predecessor, I think I consider the first one its superior. Its more fast-paced gameplay, more balanced difficulty, simpler secondary systems and tighter narrative storyline still puts Hades I above the sequel in my opinion.

Nevertheless, it's still an amazing game I can't recommend enough to both fans of action games, great stories and roguelites.
