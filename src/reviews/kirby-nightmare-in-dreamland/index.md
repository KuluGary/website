I never played Kirby games while growing up, and my first exposure to the character was in Super Smash Bros. Melee. Later in life I started to get an itch to play this game series, and after experiencing *Kirby: Star Allies* and *Kirby and the Forgotten Land* I wanted to go back an revisit some of the OGs.

Kirby: Nightmare in Dreamland is a vanilla Kirby experience; not that there's something wrong with that. This game is an upbeat adventure with a comfortable pace, tons of power-ups, and pretty graphics.

For a first-time classic Kirby experience I could have not asked for a better start.
