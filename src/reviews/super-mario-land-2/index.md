I played Super Mario Land 2 when I was a child and while I never got close to finishing it, it stuck in my mind enough to want to revisit. Playing it as an adult, this handheld Mario game is a comfortable and charming experience with upbeat music, unique level themes and good controls for a 1992 GameBoy Game.

The main con I can think aside from it's short duration is the harsh difficulty spike at Wario's Castle, which took me more attempts to beat it than I care to admin.

I would recommend this game to anyone who wants to sink a few hours in a Mario game with a low entry barrier and a unique twist into the classic formula.
