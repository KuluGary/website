I've always loved greek mythology and Hades is a fresh and modern interpretation of the well-known mythos. The deep and complex story progression and character development is only rivaled by the frantic, action packed gameplay and engaging game design.

I've spent 100+ hours on Hades in multiple savefiles and I expect to keep playing any time I want something fast and exhilarating to play.
