This is one of my most played games in my library, I've platinum'd it once, and I'm on my way to do it again. The addictive arcade gameplay makes you want to keep playing again and again, and the great amount of content makes it so it never feels repetitive.

If you like arcade games, roguelites or ginourmous messes of lights and particles, I would recommend Vampire Survivors in a heartbeat.
