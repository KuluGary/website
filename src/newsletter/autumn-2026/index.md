Hey! I hope everyone’s had a good spooky season. It’s my favorite time of year: the orange leaves, the crisp and cool air, and lots of tasty treats. After a very hot summer, I hope everyone has had a little breather for themselves.

On my end, there’s a few things to talk about that happened these past few months, so let’s get to it shall we?

## Game development

In October I participated in my first game jam, ever.

[molegato](https://molegato.com/), a developer I follow on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/molegato.com), opened a [Halloween game jam](https://molegato.com/halloween-gamejam-2026/) and myself and a handful of other developers signed up. Everyone did a really great job so please try them all.

My entry was called [Theodore’s Great Hunt](https://kulugary.itch.io/theodores-great-hunt). It’s a 10 minute riff on Duck Hunt, with a little bit of a twist added to it. If you want, please play it and share your thoughts!

I also wrote a [full-length post](https://gary.place/blog/my-first-game-jam) about it on my blog. You can read it if you’d like.


## Learning Japanese

So, uh… I’m learning Japanese? Mid-way through October I started to study Japanese through online resources. I looked around the best ways to do it, and the one study guide I found that I’m following is [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/), a website full of resources on the japanese language.
